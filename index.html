Key takeaway

Supply allocation is not only an operational calculation. It is a cross-functional decision about which objective the company will prioritize.

The central lesson

There is no universally “correct” allocation rule when functions optimize different outcomes. The company must make the governing objective explicit and manage the resulting trade-offs.

  1. 1
    Recognize competing objectives

    Revenue, profit, delivery efficiency, and customer trust can point toward different customers.

  2. 2
    Set a company-wide priority

    Leaders should decide which objective is most important under the current shortage.

  3. 3
    Communicate the trade-off

    The allocation rule and its consequences should be clear to internal teams and customers.
